# Living Laboratory: physical, author-owned paper and object permanence

**Experiment:** 2026-10-17; opt-in v5 rules stacked on v4 visible household provisions. No production/inhabited-world rewrite.

## Design objective

Pretorius should be able to *live* in a beloved and materially coherent home, not merely survive scripted hazards. Everyday freedom includes beginning projects, recording thoughts, decorating through movable objects, leaving works in progress, revisiting favorite books, and rediscovering one's own intentions. Comfort and pleasure are important outcomes. The Laboratory should supply and preserve possible actions without authoring Pretorius's personality for him.

Existing HomeLife contains actual `stationery:paper` and `stationery:ink` in the parlor writing desk, but both were inert decorative objects. The old world could not accept authored writing and the Doctor could not find it tomorrow. This is a causal limitation on independent household projects and meaningful continuity.

## Lowest-complexity mechanism

Version `pretorius-daily-rhythm/5-physical-authored-paper` adds exactly one opt-in field, `paper_marks`, to the already authoritative DailyRhythm state. A note is a bounded exact string *supplied by the acting subject*, never generated by an engineer or inserted as an autobiographical memory.

`write_paper(text)` appends an authored page only when the actual physical paper **and** ink are within reach in the actor's current room (or carried). Closed furniture obstructs access; adequate light and fatigue are required, including through the end of the five-minute writing turn. This is a small, deterministic classic-action rule—no new LLM, world database, memory organ or scheduler.

`read_paper(page)` returns the exact written text only when the actual paper can be reached, there is sufficient light, and two simulated minutes can pass. The original authenticated ledger and source references determine the page, its physical accessibility, and the exact string; any other claim is rejected.

Written text persists when paper is physically moved between rooms, cupboards or desks; it remains inaccessible in an unopened cupboard or offstage room. Restarting the SQLite world produces the same authored text. A fork inherits the current paper marks as **material condition**, not a false claim that its subsequent independent subject has personally experienced the parent's past writing events. New fork writes do not change parent paper.

The number of individual pages is bounded to 40 and each note to 240 characters, representing the actual finite stock of paper rather than unlimited magical writing capacity. Ink depletion and acquiring additional paper are future experimental questions, not falsely claimed to be implemented.

## Historical integrity

V1–v4 domestic worlds keep their original state shapes, hashes, environmental transition results, first-person frames and inability to write. V5 is only enabled in newly initialized experimental worlds. The exact original HomeLife event revision and pose are committed with each page action and reconstructed at that historical point in event replay: later moving the ink bottle must never invalidate yesterday's supported writing.

The subject receives only natural-language first person text. The Doctor Lives identity, autobiographical brain, future Village game and canonical research tracker are unchanged. Being able to *read an old note* is a real physical knowledge opportunity, not evidence of memory or autonomous cognition.

## Comparison and acceptance

The paired 48-hour experiment holds the same initial rooms, objects, physical actions, simulated clock and sleep schedule across v4 and v5. Only the new rule can mark and retrieve actual paper after the interruption. V4 receives an equal five-minute time tick as a control. Real accepted events and source hash replay are required.

The regression gate includes a source-backed first-person note, two-day rereading, moved paper, absent ink, closed desk, no reading in darkness, future-page denial, idempotent receipt ID, page capacity, restart and fork preservation, and complete original Laboratory suite on Python 3.11/3.12.

## What this does and does not establish

This is a specific independent-authorship and physical-world permanence affordance. It is not a promise that Pretorius can already perform *any* conceivable human action. Broader openness requires a reusable catalog of affordance types and natural-language-to-legal-action grounding against actual objects, not arbitrary commands that mutate a story. The next priority is to evaluate the actual Doctor's voluntary use of paper, books, comfort and household projects against a low-complexity rules baseline under equal observation budgets.

A successful unit test for the paper neither proves emotional attachment nor qualifies one of the 34 Articles. The point is to ensure that **when he decides what matters, his world can preserve it**.
